Our Story

Born in a Foundry.
Built for Champions.

The Mission

India’s Premier Gym Equipment Manufacturer

Established in 1975, Premier Rubbers is a leading Manufacturer, Wholesaler, Exporter, Supplier and Importer of a wide range of gym machines and equipment under the FitBulls brand.

We are a self-reliant company — from raw casting to finished product, everything manufactured in-house across our four production units. Automated rubber processing, kneaders, mixers, cutting, welding and moulding ensures consistent quality.

Under the leadership of Mr. Samyak Jain, we have gained a vast clientele spanning gym owners, distributors, exporters and athletes across India and beyond.

Est. 1975
Journey

Our Milestones

1975
Founded in Meerut

Premier Rubbers established in Meerut — India’s hub for sports goods manufacturing. Started with rubber gym equipment production.

1990s
Scaling Production

Expanded to multiple units. Installed automated rubber processing, kneaders, mixers, cutting, welding and moulding for scalable, consistent quality.

2000s
Export & Wholesale Growth

Became a recognized Wholesaler, Exporter and Supplier across India and internationally. GST registered: 09AABFP0071D1ZP.

2010s
FitBulls Brand Launch

Launched FitBulls — covering Phantom, TE, FLD, Rogue, Plate Loaded Series plus the complete dumbbell and weight plate range.

Today
500+ Products · Pan-India

Four manufacturing units, 500+ SKUs, growing network of gym owners, distributors and exporters. Setting the benchmark for made-in-India gym equipment.
